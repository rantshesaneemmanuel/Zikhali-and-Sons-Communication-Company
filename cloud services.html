Cloud Services

Cloud services allow people and organisations to store information, use software and access computing resources through the internet instead of relying only on their own computers.

Benefits of Cloud Services

  • Access files from different devices.
  • Store and back up important information.
  • Share files and collaborate online.
  • Reduce the need for some physical storage equipment.
