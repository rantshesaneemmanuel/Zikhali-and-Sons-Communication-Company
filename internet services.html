Internet Services

Internet services connect people, businesses and organisations to online information and communication tools. They can include broadband connections, fibre internet, wireless connections and mobile data.

Benefits of Internet Connectivity

  • Communicate through email, messaging and video calls.
  • Access educational and business information.
  • Share files and work with people remotely.
  • Use online platforms and digital services.
